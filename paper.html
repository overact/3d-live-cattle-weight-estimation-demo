PLAIN-LANGUAGE SUMMARY

Three ordinary RGB views, inspected as one reconstruction.

The paper asks whether matched left, right, and top RGB views can support useful 3D cattle reconstruction without depth, LiDAR, or stereo hardware at inference. Agreement weighting gives more influence to view updates that align with cross-view consensus and less to conflicting updates. Geometric features from the resulting point cloud then support live-weight estimation.
